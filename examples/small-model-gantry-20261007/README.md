# Checked small-model gantry geometry

This is a **geometric mock-up**, built from five separate box parts: base, left post, right post, crossbar and carriage. Qwen3:4b-instruct wrote the [box builder](box.js), [translation function](translate.js) and [assembly function](gantry.js) through Symblic, served by Ollama on a CPU-only machine. They reuse the exact previously verified rectangle, ring-join and cap functions. No candidate function, mesh or STL geometry was hand repaired. The author supplied objectives, validators, orchestration and binary STL encoding.

The target frame is 120 mm wide, 60 mm deep and 100 mm high with 8 mm members. Its 20 × 16 × 12 mm carriage sits 2 mm above the beam and is checked at x=-50,0,+50 mm. The alternate frame is 90 × 44 × 70 mm with 6 mm members, an 18 × 12 × 10 mm carriage, 1.5 mm clearance and x=-36,0,+36 mm travel checks. STL has no unit field: import coordinates as millimeters. Target center-position files: [base](target-base.stl), [left post](target-left_post.stl), [right post](target-right_post.stl), [crossbar](target-crossbar.stl), [carriage](target-carriage.stl). The bundle also contains five alternate-size STLs.

Every part has 8 vertices and 12 outward-oriented triangles. Native checks and an independent exact-rational checker cover all six assemblies: dimensions, boundary planes, surface areas, positive material volume, opposite paired edges, separate noninterpenetrating parts and the specified vertical clearance. Ten binary STLs were decoded and checked against the unchanged model meshes. Two valid and six corrupted oracle fixtures verified agreement between checkers; reversed-face mutations were rejected for all six generated assemblies.

Earlier assembly attempts include an invalid nested part shape and two timed-out provider calls; none was relabeled as successful. The final bounded assembler prompt supplied only its direct box/translation helpers while native execution still pinned and tested the entire dependency closure.

The clearance is an intentional geometric gap; this is not a complete carriage attachment or working machine design. Bearings, fasteners, joints, drives, controls, loads, material strength, tolerances and fabrication suitability are outside this example's checks. [Evidence](evidence.json) preserves new call counts, failures, hashes and original identities of reused units. It makes no general model-capability claim.

Run `python3 examples/small-model-gantry-20261007/verify.py` for independent checks. The playback program `examples/46-small-model-gantry.sym` (`tools/symblic run examples/46-small-model-gantry.sym --max-calls 0 --dir NEW_FOLDER`) executes the exact accepted functions with zero model calls and creates a separate current-runtime validation receipt.
